Redact sensitive information from PDFs—privately.

A free, private PDF redaction tool that automatically finds names, addresses, phone numbers and other sensitive information—even in scanned documents.

PDFs up to 50 MB and 100 pages. Pages that need OCR (scans, or large images): up to 20 pages and 25 MB, about 10 seconds per page. Works best on a desktop browser.

document.pdf

Your PDF is processed on this device only. It is never uploaded. No account, no tracking, no cookies.

  1. 1ScanNames, numbers, addresses and more are found automatically, with OCR for scanned pages.
  2. 2ReviewEverything found is highlighted and pre-selected. Uncheck anything that is not personal data.
  3. 3RedactSelected content is removed from the file, checked twice, and offered as a new PDF.

Detection can miss information. Names and addresses especially, and anything OCR cannot read (handwriting, faint or skewed scans). Inspect the result before sharing it. Open the downloaded file and check every page.

What does “verified” mean, and what does the tool not do?
  • Processed locally. Everything runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
  • You review first. Detected items are highlighted and pre-selected; nothing changes until you click Redact Selected Information. Uncheck anything that is not personal data.
  • What “verified” means. The tool checks that the items you selected were removed and that hidden data (metadata, comments, attachments) was stripped. It does not prove that every sensitive item was found.
